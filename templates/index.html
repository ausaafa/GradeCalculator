Quick guide

How it works

Track your course progress by saving part exam scores and chapter quiz attempts.

1
Select a course Choose your exam track to load its parts and chapters.
2
Select a part Open a part to view its overview, progress, and saved score.
3
Add your part exam score Save your main part score to reflect your overall performance in that section.
4
Select a chapter Log quiz attempts for a chapter using scores out of 10.
5
View results See your weighted grade, completion progress, and performance trend.
Reset: Clears your current selections only. Your saved grades stay saved.
